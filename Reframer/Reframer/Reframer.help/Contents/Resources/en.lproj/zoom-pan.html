Zoom & Pan

Inspect a specific part of an unlocked reference.

Zoom

  • ⇧ + scroll changes 5%.
  • ⌘⇧ + scroll changes 0.1%.
  • The zoom field accepts 10% through 1000%.
  • 0 restores 100%.

Pan

Primary-button drag anywhere on the loaded video, or use an arrow key for one point. Add ⇧ for 10 points or ⌘⇧ for 100 points. Press R to reset zoom and pan together.

Zoom, pan, reset, and opacity transformations apply to native or prepared local media. They are disabled from a pending YouTube preflight through YouTube playback.
