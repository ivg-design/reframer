Filters

Adjust the reference picture with Core Image.

Activate the filter control to choose a quick filter: Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Exposure, Edges, Sharpen, Invert, or Noir. Adjust its value when available or choose None to clear it.

Choose Advanced Filters or press F to stack effects and use Unsharp Mask, Monochrome, or Line Art. Escape closes the panel and returns focus to the invoking control.

Filter parameters are clamped and persisted.

Filters apply to native or prepared local media. They are disabled from pending YouTube preflight through YouTube playback so the embedded player remains unobscured.
