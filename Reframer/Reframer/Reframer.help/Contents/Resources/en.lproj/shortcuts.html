Scope

Playback and inspection commands require a loaded video. Pan and zoom require an unlocked overlay. Frame stepping works outside Reframer only with a loaded, locked overlay. Focused controls keep only their conventional activation and navigation keys, while text editing keeps normal field behavior.

Customize

Open shortcut settings with H. Change, clear, disable, or reset an action. Reframer rejects duplicate, reserved, modifier-collapsing, and unsafe global chords. A new chord replaces the old one and persists. Global shortcuts can be disabled independently. Reframer registers only the exact enabled global chords, so Accessibility and Input Monitoring permission are not required. If another app owns a chord, this panel reports the conflict and offers a retry action.
