Command-O, Option-Command-O, and Command-? are fixed menu/Help commands and do not appear in Shortcut Settings. Shift-scroll and Command-Shift-scroll are fixed pointer gestures. The panel's 14 editable rows cover playback, both frame directions, four pan directions, two reset actions, local and global Lock, Shortcut Settings, Close Current Panel, and Filter Panel.

Scope

Playback commands require a ready source. Pan, zoom, filters, opacity, and frame commands require native or prepared local media. Frame stepping works outside Reframer only with a loaded, locked local video whose exact or estimated sample navigation is available. Focused controls keep only their conventional activation and navigation keys, while text editing keeps normal field behavior. System file and alert sheets bypass Reframer commands, and documentation keeps native Space, arrow, and page scrolling.

Holding a toggle, panel, reset, or open shortcut does not fire it repeatedly. Frame stepping and panning are the intentional repeatable actions.

The entire overlay, including its control bar, ignores pointer input while local media is locked. Keep an enabled global lock binding so ⌘⇧L, or your customized replacement, can restore interaction from the app underneath.

Reframer refuses to lock unless that exact configured global chord is registered. If registration later disappears or conflicts, is suspended while recording a shortcut, or global shortcuts are disabled, Reframer immediately unlocks and reports recovery. Unlocking is always permitted.

Lock commands are unavailable from pending YouTube preflight through playback so the embedded player's required controls, captions, settings, fullscreen, and links remain interactive.

Customize

Open shortcut settings with H. Change, clear, disable, or reset an editable action. Fixed menu/Help commands and pointer gestures are not shown in the panel. Reframer rejects duplicate, reserved, modifier-collapsing, and unsafe global chords. A new chord replaces the old one and persists. Global shortcuts can be disabled independently. During normal operation, the enabled lock chord stays registered in every video and lock state. Frame chords are registered only in their actionable state and are not received or swallowed through the global path while another app is active. Registered hot keys require no Accessibility or Input Monitoring permission. If another app owns a chord, this panel reports the conflict and offers a retry action.

The resizable panel uses one shared five-column grid to align enabled state, shortcut, action, multiplier, and clear controls across every section. It resizes from 700×520 through 1100×1100, prefers 780×1020, remembers its validated size, and scrolls at compact heights. Tab follows row-major focus and is never recorded as a shortcut.
