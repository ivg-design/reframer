Scroll down over unlocked video to advance; scroll up to reverse. After exact indexing, navigation follows the selected video track’s decoded presentation samples. During indexing, when sample cursors are unavailable, or when a very long video exceeds the bounded exact table, the toolbar labels frame numbers as estimated.

Drag the timeline for responsive preview and release it for the nearest boundary in the active exact or estimated timeline. Playing ended media starts again at the first frame.

The latest command remains authoritative while startup, replay, or scrub seeks finish. A later Pause cannot be undone by a delayed player or seek completion. A later Play attaches to the pending seek; physical playback remains paused until the seek or scrub handoff completes.

The frame-step chords work from another app only while a video is loaded, Reframer is locked, and exact or estimated sample navigation is available. They are not registered or swallowed in any other state.
