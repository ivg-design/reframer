Lock Mode

Work in another app through the reference.

Press L or activate the lock control. The video area becomes click-through and moving, resizing, zooming, and panning are disabled. Frame, zoom, and locked-status badges remain visible and never intercept input.

When global shortcuts are enabled, ⌘⇧L toggles lock from another app. ⌘ Page Down and ⌘ Page Up step globally only while a video is loaded and Reframer is locked.

These exact chords are registered with macOS and require no Accessibility or Input Monitoring permission. Shortcut settings report registration conflicts and provide a retry action.
