Lock Mode

Work in another app through the reference.

Press L or activate the lock control. The video area becomes click-through and moving, resizing, zooming, and panning are disabled. Frame, zoom, and locked-status badges remain visible and never intercept input.

When global shortcuts are enabled, ⌘⇧L toggles lock from another app. ⌘ Page Down and ⌘ Page Up step globally only while a video is loaded, Reframer is locked, and exact or estimated sample navigation is available.

The enabled lock chord stays registered during normal operation. Frame chords are registered only in that actionable state and are removed rather than swallowed at other times. Registered hot keys require no Accessibility or Input Monitoring permission. Shortcut settings report registration conflicts and provide a retry action.
