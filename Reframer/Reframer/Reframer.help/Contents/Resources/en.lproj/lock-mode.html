Lock Mode

Work in another app through the reference.

Press L or activate the lock control. The complete overlay, including its video and control bar, uses macOS's public status-bar tier above all ordinary application windows, including normal, floating, modal, and utility windows, and becomes pointer-transparent. Moving, resizing, zooming, and panning are disabled. Frame, zoom, and locked-status badges remain visible and never intercept input.

Critical system pop-up menus, drag UI, the screen saver, and assistive-technology windows remain above the locked overlay.

Lock mode forces its status-bar tier even when Always on Top When Unlocked is off. Because pointer input also passes through the control bar, use the enabled global lock shortcut—⌘⇧L by default—to restore interaction from the app underneath. Unlocking returns to the saved unlocked-state preference.

Reframer refuses to lock unless the exact configured global Lock/Unlock chord is registered. If registration later disappears or conflicts, is suspended while recording a shortcut, or global shortcuts are disabled, Reframer automatically unlocks and reports recovery. Unlocking is always permitted.

⌘ Page Down and ⌘ Page Up step globally only while a video is loaded, Reframer is locked, and exact or estimated sample navigation is available.

The enabled lock chord stays registered during normal operation. Frame chords are registered only in that actionable state and are removed rather than swallowed at other times. Registered hot keys require no Accessibility or Input Monitoring permission. Shortcut settings report registration conflicts and provide a retry action.
