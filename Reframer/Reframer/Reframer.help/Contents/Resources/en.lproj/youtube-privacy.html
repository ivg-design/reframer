YouTube Privacy and Terms

What happens when you explicitly load a YouTube link.

Before the player opens

Reframer sends the parsed video identifier to the YouTube Data API to retrieve the current Made for Kids status. The identifier is in the request query and the API key is sent in the X-Goog-Api-Key header, rather than the URL. Google also necessarily receives ordinary HTTPS request and network metadata such as your IP address. If that required check fails or returns no status, Reframer does not load the embedded player.

Player privacy

After approval, Reframer loads YouTube's privacy-enhanced embedded player in an ephemeral system WebKit view. YouTube receives network information such as your IP address, user agent, Reframer's app identity, and the selected video identifier. YouTube may display content, controls, links, and ads. The WebKit data store is not persisted and is cleared before every embed. Session cookies or player data can exist while that player runs, but are never persisted. Reframer does not retain or log the pasted URL or viewing history.

The player does not autoplay. YouTube selects adaptive quality; its supported embed API does not let Reframer force maximum quality. Standard controls, links, branding, ads, settings, and WebKit element fullscreen remain available. The web view fills the complete video canvas and remains at least 200×200 points; YouTube owns internal aspect fit and letterboxing. Reframer disables filters and transforms from pending preflight through playback. It also unlocks and disables click-through Lock so controls, captions, settings, fullscreen, and links remain interactive. Use Always on Top When Unlocked for a topmost YouTube window.

Your choice

YouTube is contacted only after you paste a link, review this notice, and select Load Video. You can stop playback or replace the source at any time. Reframer stores only the accepted notice version locally. It does not store the pasted link, viewing history, or YouTube login credentials.

Terms

By using YouTube playback in Reframer, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
