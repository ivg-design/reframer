Getting Started

Turn a local video into a reference overlay.

  1. Activate the empty-state Open action, drop a file, or press ⌘O.
  2. Choose an MP4, M4V, or MOV file.
  3. Press Space to play or pause.
  4. Use ⌘ Page Down and ⌘ Page Up to step.
  5. Drag the control-bar grip to move the window, or drag the video to pan.
  6. Set opacity, size, and zoom. Frame, zoom, and lock badges stay visible.
  7. Press L to make the video click-through.

Reframer checks that macOS can decode the chosen video track before presenting the asset as ready.
